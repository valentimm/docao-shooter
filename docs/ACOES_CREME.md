# Ações em creme sobre verde

Artes transparentes editadas com a ferramenta nativa image_gen. Arquivos: `lettering-juntar-turma-creme.png` e `lettering-trocar-visual-creme.png`. Versões anteriores preservadas.

## JUNTAR A TURMA

Edit this transparent cartoon lettering asset. Recolor ONLY the dark brown letters into LIGHT WARM IVORY CREAM (#fff0cc), with shallow tan edge #d9b77e and clean dark forest-green outline #172d22. The letters will appear ON A DARK FOREST GREEN button, so all letter faces must be very light and readable. Preserve the exact text "JUNTAR A TURMA", same letter silhouettes, size, spacing, line breaks, canvas proportions and transparent framing as reference. Do not redesign letterforms. Keep broad smooth cartoon fills and restrained shallow relief, no realistic texture, no glossy effects, no heavy shadow. No gold or yellow faces. GENUINELY TRANSPARENT background and clean alpha around lettering; no button, no plaque, no background, no other words or icons. Color variation only.
